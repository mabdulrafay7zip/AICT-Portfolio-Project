My Short Story

I am Rafay — an AI student who believes the best way to learn machine learning is to build things that work, not just read about them.

My journey started at Air University, Islamabad, where I am pursuing my BS in Artificial Intelligence. What began with programming fundamentals quickly turned into a fascination with data — cleaning it, questioning it, and teaching machines to find patterns in it.

Since then I have completed three machine learning internships, trained models that reach up to 97.5% accuracy, and shipped live projects — including a Netflix recommender that anyone in the world can open and try right now.
